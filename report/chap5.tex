\chapter{Conclusion and Further Work}

\section{Conclusion}
COBRA-WATCH addresses the blind spot between network vulnerability posture and physical camera feed authenticity through Integrity-Gated Dual-Channel Trust Fusion (IG-DCTF). Rather than allowing clean software metadata or patched CVE histories to endorse compromised video streams, the scoring engine joins Channel~A vulnerability posture ($T_{\mathrm{cve}} \in [0, 100]$) with Channel~B signal authenticity ($s = (1 - \mathrm{Drift})\,L \in [0, 1]$) through a piece-wise multiplicative gate ($T_{\mathrm{final}} = \mathrm{round}(T_{\mathrm{cve}} \cdot G(s))$). Whenever stream integrity drops below $\theta_{\mathrm{low}} = 0.50$, the gate enforces an explicit floor multiplier of $0.30$ and hard-caps the final alert score at \texttt{low\_trust} ($T_{\mathrm{final}} \le 30$) regardless of CVE posture, while tolerating benign single-field firmware changes above $0.50$.

Operating within India's IT Act, 2000 (\S43/\S66) and DPDP Act, 2023 boundaries---with zero outbound probing modules, strict non-whitelisted RTSP refusal, and \texttt{/24} UI masking---the Review~2 implementation establishes three grounded engineering deliverables:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Non-Attributive OSINT Exposure Aggregate:} Under Censys research access (\texttt{RS-469}), \texttt{data/censys/2026-11-26/snapshot.json} records \textbf{104,218} exposed Indian camera hosts across six aggregate dimensions (\textbf{81,285} advertising RTSP port~554) with zero raw target IP persistence or redistribution.
  \item \textbf{Frozen Tier~2 Detector (\texttt{v2}) on Public CUHK Avenue Clips:} Evaluated against un-tuned \texttt{v1}, frozen \texttt{v2} (\texttt{023c5c4}) improves held-out replay-loop detection from \textbf{1/16} to \textbf{15/16} ($93.8\%$), detects \textbf{16/16} simulated incidents across \texttt{freeze}, \texttt{blur}, \texttt{slow\_blur}, \texttt{cover}, \texttt{partial\_cover}, \texttt{moved}, and \texttt{downscale}, reduces \texttt{tripped} clean false alarms from \textbf{4} ($33.5$/h) to \textbf{0} (\textbf{0.0/h} $[0.0, 30.9]$; \textbf{1} \texttt{degraded} warning = $8.4$/h), holds up on unseen \texttt{loop\_2s/10s} ($15/16$), \texttt{blur\_sigma2/4/8} ($16/16$), \texttt{slow\_blur\_20s} ($16/16$), and \texttt{cover\_semi\_transparent} ($16/16$) while exposing boundaries on \texttt{partial\_cover\_small} ($0/16$), \texttt{loop\_30s} ($10/16$), and \texttt{slow\_blur\_120s} ($13/16$ \texttt{tripped}), and sustains \textbf{242.7~FPS} ($320$~px) and \textbf{59.9~FPS} ($640$~px) on real decoded Avenue frames on CPU.
  \item \textbf{Full-Stack Passive Triage \& Forensic Pipeline:} FastAPI REST/WebSocket ingestion (API key auth, 5-minute idempotency, 10/min rate limits, 60~s timestamp checks), SHA-256 hash-chained \texttt{audit\_ledger}, Next.js~16 operator console (\texttt{/}, \texttt{/research}, \texttt{/lab}, \texttt{/ethics}, \texttt{/console}) backed by clearly labelled simulated fixtures (\texttt{9,320} rows in \texttt{data/public.db}), and consistent Python (\texttt{440} collected; \texttt{439} passed, \texttt{1} skipped prior to final test run) plus Vitest (\texttt{10} suites, \texttt{76} passed on 2026-11-28) coverage tracked centrally in \texttt{frontend/src/lib/content/facts.manual.ts}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}
Honest interpretation of Review~2 results requires stating five structural limitations directly:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Same Camera, Same Scene, Co-Developed Attack Generator, and Unseen Sweep Failures:} Although CUHK Avenue splits \texttt{training\_videos} and \texttt{testing\_videos}, both sets depict the exact same fixed outdoor walkway camera under uniform daylight, and the tamper transforms were generated programmatically (\texttt{eval/tier2\_real\_footage.py}). Moreover, our unseen 10-variant robustness sweep on Avenue exposes genuine detector limits without modifying \texttt{v2}: small partial occlusions ($\approx 9\%$ corner/side cover, \texttt{partial\_cover\_small}) go undetected (\textbf{0/16}, under the $12/64$ block rule), $30$~s loops exceed the $18$~s ($450$-frame) ring buffer (\textbf{10/16}), and $120$~s slow focus ramps reach \texttt{tripped} on only \textbf{13/16} shorter clips (\texttt{16/16} at \texttt{degraded}).
  \item \textbf{Short Clean Windows and Dropped Clips:} Requiring at least $12$~s per clip excluded \textbf{2/16} training clips and \textbf{5/21} held-out test clips, leaving only $0.1306$~h (training) and $0.1195$~h ($\approx 7.2$~min, held-out) of clean footage. Zero \texttt{tripped} false alarms over $0.1195$~h still carries an exact 95\% Poisson upper confidence bound of \textbf{30.9/h} ($[0.0, 30.9]$), while \texttt{degraded} false alarms stand at $8.4$/h ($[0.2, 46.6]$) due to \texttt{video~06}.
  \item \textbf{Absence of Real Longitudinal Tier~1 Data and Owned Day/Night Clips:} Passive banner drift relies on \texttt{100} synthetic mutations spanning only \textbf{13} distinct baseline--current pairs, and self-recorded day/night \texttt{Own clips} across distinct camera scenes and night illumination have not yet been captured and evaluated.
  \item \textbf{Limitations of the $n=120$ Sanity Corpus and Weight Revision:} The 120-scenario scoring corpus was authored by the team (\texttt{90} posture-only cases follow intended tier labels), cannot support significance tests, leaves a flat $F_1 = 0.9756$ across all $42$ threshold grid pairs (so it does not test $(0.50, 0.85)$), and prompted an explicit post-inspection revision of Tier~2 sub-weights from $(0.5, 0.3, 0.2)$ to $(1.0, 0.6, 0.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Storage Concurrency:} Single-file SQLite WAL limits concurrent multi-writer deployment beyond single-host demonstration scale (all three container images build cleanly via \texttt{docker compose build}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Future Scope}
To close the remaining generalisation and operational gaps after Review~2, planned next stages are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Owned Day and Night Scene Evaluation (\texttt{Own clips}):} Record ethical, privacy-clean $60$~s real clips across distinct camera angles and low-light night conditions (containing no faces or private spaces) and run both standard and unseen-variant sweeps for \texttt{v1} vs.\ frozen \texttt{v2}.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Scale Block \& Extended Loop Horizon (Post-Review~2):} Address the sweep failure modes discovered on frozen \texttt{v2} by extending loop buffer retention beyond $18$~s and adding finer sub-block occlusion triggers while keeping false-alarm calibration intact on night scenes.
  \item \textbf{Empirical Tier~1 Drift Calibration \& Adaptive Adversaries:} Collect multi-month Censys aggregate and authorized-device histories to measure real firmware turnover versus MITM banner drift, calibrate conformal threshold margins, and evaluate adaptive adversaries injecting per-frame sensor jitter against pHash ring buffers.
  \item \textbf{Production Scalability \& Multi-Host Rehearsal:} Migrate SQLite storage to PostgreSQL with Redis-backed worker queues and maintain two-laptop live E2E rehearsals.
\end{enumerate}
